# Faculty Research #
This project relates to a newer method of measuring blood flow, touching areas in photonics and biomedical engineering. Here we list a few professors and their research that relate to this project:
1. Darren Roblyer, PhD (https://www.bu.edu/eng/profile/darren-roblyer-ph-d/) with BU's Biomedical Optical Technologies Lab (https://www.bu.edu/botlab/). One of the most relevant papers is "Speckle contrast optical spectroscopy for cuffless blood pressure estimation based on microvascular blood flow and volume oscillations" (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40809974/), which is a more complex and higher-budget version of a SCOS setup. Our goal is to imitate this but with simplified, accessible hardware on a smaller scale.
